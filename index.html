How it works

Three steps. Zero trust required.

  1. Step 01 · The frame

    We read the glass.

    The GlassProof SDK runs on the screen's media player and reads the framebuffer — the exact image sent to the display. No cameras required. No creative modification. No cooperation from the ad stack.

  2. Step 02 · The match

    The ad verifies itself.

    Frames are fingerprinted on-device using IRCODE's production visual-recognition engine — proven across 1.5M+ real-world scans in 52 countries — and matched against registered creatives. The image is the code: your creative is its own verification token.

  3. Step 03 · The receipt

    Recorded. Independent. Undeniable.

    Every verified play becomes an independent, timestamped record — creative, screen, venue, duration, display-power state — reported the moment it happens to the GlassProof platform, outside the reach of anyone's edits. That's what makes it stand up to any audit.

GlassProof is built on IRCODE — patented computer vision that turns any creative into its own identifier: still images, full-motion video spots, even live broadcast. No QR codes. No watermarks. No changes to the creative. The picture itself is the code.

For your engineers

Under the hood.

The complete loop, end to end. Your CTO will want the details — here they are.

  1. 01 · REGISTER

    Creatives are registered with IREngine — IRCODE's recognition backend — through the GlassProof API. Stills and video are fingerprinted into compact visual signatures (video across its full timeline) and indexed for instant retrieval. Nothing is embedded in the file, and signatures are one-way: the ad can't be reconstructed from them.

  2. 02 · SAMPLE

    The GlassProof SDK runs natively on the screen's media player — BrightSign, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android — and reads frames from the framebuffer: the exact image being sent to the glass, after the CMS and player have done their work. Under 1% CPU. Multi-zone layouts are fingerprinted per zone.

  3. 03 · MATCH

    Frames are fingerprinted on-device and matched in under a millisecond against a local index of active campaigns, with IREngine cloud lookup for the long tail. Only signatures ever leave the device — never images, never audience data. Video is tracked second-by-second through the spot's timeline, so full duration is measured, not assumed.

  4. 04 · RECORD

    Every verified play becomes a record — creative, version, screen, venue, timestamps, duration coverage, display state — created on the device and reported in real time to the GlassProof platform. The operator's systems never touch it, so no one in the transaction can edit history after the fact. Offline screens buffer records locally and sync in order on reconnect.

  5. 05 · REPORT

    Records stream into logged-vs-verified reconciliation, certification status, and APIs that plug into CMS billing and SSP deal IDs. Every dashboard number resolves to the underlying play records your team can inspect, export, and re-check independently.
